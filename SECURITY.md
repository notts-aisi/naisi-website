# Security

This repository is the website of the University of Nottingham AI Safety
Initiative (naisi.uk). The site holds members' personal details, so a report
about a weakness is welcome and will be read.

## Reporting a problem

Please report privately, not in a public issue or pull request.

- Preferred: [open a private report on GitHub](https://github.com/notts-aisi/naisi-website/security/advisories/new).
  Only the maintainers can see it.
- Or email ai-safety@uonsu.com with "Security" in the subject line.

Tell us what you found, where (a URL or a file), and how to reproduce it.
Please do not include other people's personal data: a description of what
could be read is enough.

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge a report within 7 days.
- We will tell you what we found, and when a fix is live.
- The site is run by student volunteers, so there is no bounty.

## Ground rules

- Use your own account. Do not read, change or delete anyone else's data.
- Do not send email or notifications to other people through the site, and do
  not flood it with requests.
- Stop once you have shown the problem exists, then report it.

If you stay within these, we will treat your report as help.

## Scope

In scope: naisi.uk, dev.naisi.uk and the code in this repository.

Out of scope: the services the site runs on (Google Cloud, Firebase,
Cloudflare, GitHub, Resend), which take reports themselves, and anything that
needs physical access to a member's device.
